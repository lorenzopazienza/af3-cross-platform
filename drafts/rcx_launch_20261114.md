### During the runs

- **Stop rule.** If `results/rcx_cap_stop.txt` appears, the wrapper that hit it starts no further part (running parts
  finish) and the supervisor relaunches nothing. Stop that wrapper with Ctrl-C (its retry rounds would only be refused
  again), and decide in a section 10 row. Every guard decision is in `results/rcx_guard.tsv`.
- **Gate of part A3.** When every part A4 part has a complete session, the supervisor runs
  `credit_check_rcx.sh l4` with the balance estimated as 87 USD minus follow-up 15's spend (the console balance lags
  the spend) and, on GO only, starts the L4 wrapper of the next block. On NO GO it exits and nothing is launched.
- **HARD or CAPPED in the supervisor log**: a part failed after AlphaFold 3 had started, or has had 6 VMs; you decide.
