(a) Question: does the platform change the quality of the structure AlphaFold 3 returns? For each paired prediction (target, seed) of a contrast, the top-ranked sample of side A and the top-ranked sample of side B are each compared with the experimental structure, and the two qualities are differenced. (b) Data, all frozen: the per-sample tables and seed pairs of the main study (`results/analysis/main_20261014/primary_v2`, `samples.csv` and `contrasts/pairs_seed.csv`), follow-up 4 (`cpu60_20261018`), follow-up 13 (`fu13_20261112/samples.csv`, V6f and Gf) and follow-up 14 (`fu14_20261113b`). No session is collected and no output is rescored. Contrasts: the three primary pairs HW(V5,V6), HW(V6,G) and HW(G,C) of the main study (HW(G,C) on the 6 targets and seeds 1 and 2 that C ran); HW(G,C) on the 60 targets of follow-up 4; the ten float32 pairs HW(Pf,Qf) of follow-up 14 (four primary, six secondary); as yardsticks, RC(G) and SD(V6) of the main study and RC(Gf). Sides as registered: HW(P,Q) is P r1 against Q r1 on the same seed, RC(P) is r1 against r2, and SD(P) is seed s against seed s' of P r1. The top-1 of each side is the registered one (`top1_a`, `top1_b` of `pairs_seed.csv`). (c) Measures: the top-1 sample's M1 ligand RMSD from the experimental structure (`m1_ligand_rmsd`, angstrom, after pocket superposition), its ipTM and its mean pLDDT, each as the paired difference d = B minus A, A the first-named side. A unit without the measure on either side leaves that measure's statistics and is counted. Strata: every paired unit ("all", primary) and the units whose registered top-1 differs ("flipped", beside it; not for SD, whose sample indices are unrelated draws). (d) Statistics: median of d (primary) and mean of d, each with a 95% percentile interval from the target bootstrap (`analysis/contrasts.boot_ci`: targets resampled with replacement, the units of a drawn target pooled, 10,000 replicates, a fresh generator from seed 2026 for every statistic, so every statistic sees the same resampled targets); also the median of the absolute d. No correction for multiple comparisons. (e) Reading rule, ligand RMSD, median of d, stratum "all" (the "flipped" stratum is read the same way, beside it): the platform returns poses farther from the experimental structure on B if the interval lies above 0, closer if below 0, and "not distinguishable" otherwise; in addition, "within 0.1 A" if the whole interval lies in [-0.1, 0.1] A. The band is 5% of the 2 A success threshold, chosen here before any value. ipTM and mean pLDDT: the same three-way reading, descriptive, without a band. (f) Checks (the analysis stops if 1 or 2 fails): 1, every registered top-1 names exactly one row of the per-sample table; 2, that row is the argmax of the full-precision ranking score of its (condition, repetition, target, seed) under `contrasts.top`, and its `m1_success` equals the registered success; 3, reported, the units without each measure per contrast and side.
