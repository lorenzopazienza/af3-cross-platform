## Paper limitation sentence for follow-up 10

"Every target has at most 1,024 tokens, so we cannot say whether hardware-induced changes grow for larger inputs:
under our selection rule (one protein chain and one ligand from PoseBusters V2) a single complex lies between 1,100
and 2,000 tokens, and widening the rule to multi-chain complexes would confound input size with chain count. Within
the range we measured, the per-seed top-1 flip rate by token bucket is reported as an exploratory analysis (Table
T16a)."
