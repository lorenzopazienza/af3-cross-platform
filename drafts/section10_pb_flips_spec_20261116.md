| 2026-11-16 | Post hoc analysis of frozen outputs, which success criterion changes in a success flip and by how much (label `pb_flips`), specified after the main-study and follow-up 1 to 15 outcomes and the post hoc analyses `returned`, `rare`, `rounded`, `winners` and `cpu_matched` were read, and before any value of this analysis was computed; descriptive whatever it shows, outside the pre-registered study. (a) Question: are success flips changes of pose, or crossings of a threshold near its limit? For every success flip (the registered top-1 of one side succeeds and the other's does not), which criterion of M1 fails on the failing side (the 2 A RMSD threshold, a PoseBusters check and which, or both), and how far the failing value lies beyond its threshold or tolerance. (b) Data, all frozen and local: the per-sample tables and seed pairs of the main study (`results/analysis/main_20261014/primary_v2`: HW(V5,V6), HW(V6,G), RC(G)) and of follow-ups 3 and 9 as `analysis/winners.py` loads them (`h100_20261104/pairs_seed.csv`: HW(H,A), RC(H), RC(A); H from `h100_20261104/samples.csv`, A from `a100_20261018/samples.csv`); the saved M1 results of the two top-1 samples (`m1_ligand_rmsd`, `m1_pb_valid`, `m1_pb_failed`, `m1_success`, `m1_error`); and the top-1 mmCIF files of both sides of every success flip under `results/af3/` (41 flips: 11, 10, 5, 6, 5 and 4; 73 distinct files, all local). Groups: "platform" (HW(V5,V6), HW(V6,G), HW(H,A)) and "recompilation" (RC(G), RC(H), RC(A)). (c) Measures. From the saved results (no rerun needed): the failing side and the criterion that fails on it, "RMSD" (ligand RMSD of 2 A or more, PoseBusters-valid), "PoseBusters" (below 2 A, not valid), "RMSD and PoseBusters", or "not scored" (an M1 error); the PoseBusters checks that fail. Success needs both criteria, so nothing fails on the succeeding side. From a rerun on the 73 files only: M1 is recomputed by the registered function `score_ligand.score` (posebusters 0.6.5, configuration "dock", the predicted protein in the superposed frame as `mol_cond`; the analysis stops if posebusters is another version), then the same steps once more with PoseBusters' full report (`bust(full_report=True)`), which gives the continuous value behind each of the 16 checks that have one (bond lengths, bond angles and internal clash as the largest relative deviation from the distance-geometry bound; the two ring planarity values and the double-bond planarity in A; the energy ratio; the smallest ligand-protein distance in A; the smallest distance over the sum of van der Waals radii for protein, organic cofactors, inorganic cofactors and waters; the four volume overlaps), against its threshold from `dock.yml` (0.25, 0.25, 0.3, 0.25, 0.05, 0.25, 100, 5 A, 0.75 and 0.075). The six other checks (loading, sanitization, InChI, connectivity, radicals) are binary and have no distance. Excess over the tolerance, as a fraction of the threshold: (value minus threshold) over threshold for an upper limit, (threshold minus value) over threshold for a lower limit, above 0 fails; for the RMSD, (RMSD minus 2 A) over 2 A, 0 or above fails. Per flip: the excess of each failing criterion on the failing side and the worst (largest) of them; the excess of the same criteria on the succeeding side (how far inside); and the ligand RMSD between the two top-1 samples (`winners.pose_rmsd_real`, the pose function of M4 and `winners`). Classification, chosen here before any value: "pose change" if the two top-1 poses are more than 2 A apart (the "distinct" of `winners`), whatever the excess; otherwise "near-threshold crossing" if every criterion that fails on the failing side exceeds its tolerance by at most 10% of the threshold (for the RMSD, below 2.2 A); otherwise "other"; "not classified" if a needed value is missing (a failing binary check, a pose RMSD that cannot be computed, a check without a value). Strata: all success flips, and beside it the flips whose top-1 sample changed (9 of the 41 keep the same sample index and change its coordinates). (d) Statistics per contrast and group: counts per criterion, per failing check and per class; the shares of "near-threshold crossing" and of "pose change" among the classified flips, and the median worst excess of the failing side, each with a 95% percentile interval from the bootstrap over targets (`contrasts.boot_ci`, 10,000 replicates, a fresh generator from seed 2026 for every statistic; in a group, the flips of a target in several contrasts are pooled under that target). The per-flip table is reported in full. No correction for multiple comparisons. (e) Reading: descriptive; the classification is the only rule, and the platform group is read beside the recompilation group. (f) Checks: 1 and 2 as in `winners` (every registered top-1 names one row of its per-sample table, is the argmax of the ranking score under `contrasts.top` and has the registered success); 3, the rerun reproduces the saved M1 exactly on every one of the 73 files (the ligand RMSD as written, to 4 decimals, the validity, the set of failed checks, the success and the error); 3b, the full report gives the same failed checks as the standard report; 4, the sign of every computed excess agrees with the outcome of its check on every file.
