## Row B: two arms in parallel

| 2026-11-0X | Launch setting, not a change in any computation, recorded before any follow-up 11 or 12 run: "one arm at a time" (rows of 2026-10-29 and 2026-11-03) becomes at most one TPU arm and one GPU arm at a time, never two arms on one platform. Both arms are launched from the one launch host, the Mac, from the main checkout. Each arm, and from now on every arm, is launched with `~/Desktop/af3_drafts/retry_arm_par.sh`, which reads the summary of its own wrapper run instead of the newest one, and with `ALLOW_OTHER_LAUNCHERS=1`. Without that setting `cloud/af3_run_many.sh` refuses to start while any `af3_run.sh` of another arm runs; a round of either wrapper would then do nothing and wait 20 min, up to 12 times. GPU launchers in total stay within the GPUS_ALL_REGIONS quota of 8: while H or H r2 runs (3 H100 at a time, `MAX_H100=3`), an L4 arm runs with `MAX_L4=5`; otherwise `MAX_L4=8`. TPU arms run one part at a time (`MAX_V5E=1`, `MAX_V6E=1`, the defaults). The budget rule of each follow-up subtracts the expected cost of every arm still running (`credit_check_fu11_12.sh`). Order within each family: TPU V5r, V6r, then V6h5; GPU Gr, then Gh5. Follow-up 12 starts after every follow-up 11 arm has finished, as its row says. H r2 is launched with `retry_arm_par.sh` too, so a follow-up 11 GPU arm can run beside it. No second arm starts while an arm launched with the old `retry_arm.sh` runs (H): that script reads the newest `.many` folder, which would then belong to the other arm. Checked for shared state between two concurrent wrappers (scripts read, 2026-11-03): run folders `results/af3/.many/<UTC second>/` (distinct unless two wrappers start a round in the same second; the second then stops with "exists" and retries 20 min later); part locks `results/af3/.many/locks/<platform>_<plan>` (plan names distinct per arm); session and VM names (UTC second plus platform and plan; distinct across platforms); temporary files (`mktemp`); quota counters (per wrapper, hence the platform rule and `MAX_L4`). Nothing else is shared. | Two arms at once, one per platform family, keep the calendar within 2026-11-14 |

## Row C: follow-up 10 on Runs N' Poses, feasibility (a record, not a specification)

Fewer than 15 large targets remain under the proposed sequence clustering, so no specification row is drafted. This
row records the screen, and the Limitations sentence below replaces the follow-up.

| 2026-11-04 | Follow-up 10 (label `large`), feasibility screen on Runs N' Poses, made before any input was built or any run; no study output read. Source: `data/runs_n_poses/annotations.csv` (2,600 systems). Screen: `targets/large/rnp_screen.py`, run in the AlphaFold3 environment. It applies the main study's rule: one protein chain in the system and exactly one proper ligand chain (other ligands of the system left out, as the main study leaves out cofactors); no nucleic acid in the entry; the RCSB canonical sequence of the chain's entity (RCSB Data API, cached), polypeptide(L), 20 standard amino acids; the ligand in AlphaFold3's CCD. The input (chain plus ligand by CCD code, empty MSA, no templates) is featurised by AlphaFold3 for exact token counts. Of 1,531 systems with one protein chain and one proper ligand, 1,497 are eligible. Exclusions: nucleic acid in the entry 19, ligand not in the CCD 7, non-standard letters 7, entry not found 1. By AlphaFold3 bucket: 256: 251, 512: 987, 768: 195, 1,024: 41, 1,280: 19, 1,536: 4. Released 2021-10-06 to 2024-05-29, all after the training cutoff. 27 eligible systems are entries among the 60 or the 72 and are left out. Sequence clusters: the annotations' `cluster` column is the SuCOS-pocket cluster of the system (Runs N' Poses documentation), not a sequence cluster. Proposed method: greedy clustering at 40% identity (identical aligned residues over the shorter sequence, global alignment with free end gaps, BLOSUM62), 40% being the identity at which AlphaFold3 clusters protein chains for training-set sampling (`~/Desktop/af3_drafts/fu10_rnp/rnp_clusters.py`). Large group (1,025 to 1,536 tokens): 23 systems; one per 40% cluster, 11 (bucket 1,280: 7, 1,536: 4). Other thresholds: 10 at 30%, 12 at 90%, 16 with identical sequences only. Size-control pool (at most 1,024 tokens, one per cluster, clusters not in the large group): 570. Decision: with 11 independent large targets, one target is about 9 points of any rate, and an interval for the difference between groups would not be readable; follow-up 10 is not run, and the Limitations sentence states it. | Too few independent large targets in Runs N' Poses |
